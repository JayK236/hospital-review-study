Ratings by hospital type

Strongest predictor

Hospital type explains the most variance (R² ≈ 0.23–0.31). Kaiser facilities score 1.7–2.3 stars above public safety-net hospitals after controlling for reviewer characteristics.

Role & gender effects

Replicates across markets

Patients rate higher than caretakers. Male caretakers are the most negative group — the male deficit largely disappears among direct patients. This gender × role interaction replicates independently in both SF (p = 0.009) and Sacramento (p < 0.001).

Review length effect

Longer reviews consistently predict lower ratings (coef ≈ −0.001/character, p < 0.001 in all models). Consistent with longer reviews reflecting higher emotional arousal or negative experiences.
